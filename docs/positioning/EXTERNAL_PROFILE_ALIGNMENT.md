## Completion definition

External profile alignment is complete when:

- LinkedIn, X, GitHub, and the portfolio share one primary identity;
- the MedPiper role and proof points are consistent across surfaces;
- bounded commercial work is not mistaken for current employment or a generic agency;
- direct product evidence is featured only after approval;
- social previews and search snippets are manually inspected;
- stale headlines, old “full-stack AI builder” language, and unsupported claims are removed;
- the resulting profiles are judged through actual recruiter/founder comprehension, not internal preference alone.
