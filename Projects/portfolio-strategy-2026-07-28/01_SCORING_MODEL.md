# Scoring model

## Why a single score is insufficient

Each project is judged on four outcomes:

- **Cash:** probability of reaching paid work quickly.
- **Equity:** probability of becoming a recurring, scalable and defensible product.
- **Career:** signal for senior product, AI, domain and technical opportunities.
- **Creative value:** learning, public identity and sustained personal energy.

The `Capital Score` is a concentration heuristic, not a valuation. A project can have low near-term capital priority and high career or creative value.

## Positive factors

Each factor is scored 1–5 and weighted:

| Factor | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Pain urgency/frequency | 13 | Is this costly, risky or repeatedly frustrating? |
| Buyer clarity/budget | 12 | Is there a named buyer with authority and budget? |
| GTM reachability | 11 | Can Pranay reach enough qualified buyers without a large brand or paid channel? |
| Speed to paid proof | 11 | Can a bounded paid result be sold in weeks, not quarters? |
| Recurrence/retention | 10 | Does the job repeat enough to support recurring revenue? |
| Defensibility/compounding | 9 | Does usage create data, workflow lock-in, expertise or distribution advantage? |
| Founder/domain fit | 10 | Does Pranay have credibility, insight or access unavailable to a generic builder? |
| Existing evidence | 8 | Is there working product, live use, benchmarks or customer-adjacent proof? |
| Scalability/margin | 8 | Can value grow faster than human delivery cost? |
| Career signal | 8 | Does it materially improve high-value client, partner or role credibility? |

## Risk penalties

Execution drag, regulatory/liability risk and commoditization are scored 1–5 and subtract up to 20 points. This prevents large, impressive or trendy projects from receiving a false ranking.

## Evidence ladder

| Tier | Evidence |
|---|---|
| E0 | idea, research or internal enthusiasm |
| E1 | working code, tests, benchmark or internal use |
| E2 | live public product or external users |
| E3 | repeated problem interviews and supplied real data |
| E4 | paid diagnostic or paid pilot |
| E5 | repeat purchase, recurring use, reference and repeatable acquisition |

Most of the portfolio is E1–E2. PMF language is therefore inappropriate.

## Decision thresholds

- **75+**: focus candidate, but only with explicit role and next evidence gate.
- **60–74**: paid-validation or customer-gated option.
- **45–59**: subsystem, portfolio or maintenance asset.
- **Below 45**: pause/archive unless personal or creative value is deliberately funded.

Scores must be updated when evidence changes. A paid pilot matters more than twenty new features.
