# Capital allocation and 90-day plan

## Portfolio rules

1. Maximum three commercial workstreams at once.
2. Only one workstream may be in broad build mode; the others must be selling, delivering or measuring.
3. A project without a named next evidence gate receives zero backlog work.
4. Customer-gated means no architecture expansion before real data or payment.
5. The 5% creative allocation is a ceiling, not a minimum backlog entitlement.

## 90-day objective

Reach **three paid diagnostics/pilots, two references and one repeated workflow** while preserving the long-term insurance product thesis.

## Days 1–15: package and sell

### DocumentOps
- Select two verticals: CA/accounting operations and insurance/benefits servicing.
- Create one fixed diagnostic with deliverables, price and data-handling boundary.
- Record one six-minute before/after workflow using a realistic packet.
- Build a 100-account list with named operators, not generic company emails.

### SignKit
- Clarify “visual signature placement” versus certified e-signature.
- Introduce individual and professional/batch tiers.
- Ship vertical templates and a short proof video.

### insurance_q
- Rename.
- Remove or gate recommendation-like surfaces.
- Run five real policy packets and document every abstention/correction.

### Waypoint
- Reduce the demo to inquiry → structured packet → missing questions → follow-up draft.
- Contact 50 boutique agencies.

## Days 16–45: paid proof

Targets:
- 20 qualified DocumentOps conversations.
- 5 diagnostic proposals.
- 2 paid DocumentOps diagnostics.
- 10 insurance institutional interviews and 3 real document sets.
- 10 Waypoint demos and 1 paid pilot.
- 10 SignKit paid users from targeted, not broad, traffic.

Engineering is limited to blockers discovered during these proofs.

## Days 46–90: standardize or kill

- Turn repeated DocumentOps steps into a canonical workflow, reusable components and a margin model.
- Convert one diagnostic into an implementation/retainer.
- Obtain one paid insurance proof or formally keep insurance_q in design-partner mode.
- Decide whether Waypoint remains the third bet based on paid usage.
- Replace Waypoint with AdShot or SentinelTwin only if one has stronger paid evidence.

## Offer hypotheses

### DocumentOps diagnostic
- Inputs: sample packet, current steps, volume, errors, turnaround, data constraints.
- Outputs: workflow map, risk points, automation boundary, sample transformed packet, implementation quote.
- Price test: ₹25,000 / ₹45,000 / ₹60,000.

### DocumentOps implementation
- One workflow, one operator role, one primary document type, human review, audit trail.
- Price test: ₹75,000–₹3,00,000 setup plus support/managed operations.

### Insurance proof-of-value
- One policy family or benefit document set.
- Evidence-linked summary/Q&A or old-new change pack.
- Price test: ₹2,00,000–₹8,00,000 depending on scope and review.

### Waypoint pilot
- One inbox/source, one agency, inquiry packet and follow-up draft.
- Global price test: US$1,000–US$3,000 setup plus US$200–US$750/month.

## Stop conditions

Stop or redesign an offer when any of these occurs:
- fewer than 3 of 20 qualified prospects acknowledge urgent pain;
- no buyer will pay for a bounded diagnostic;
- customization exceeds 30% of delivery effort after the third customer;
- users do not repeat the workflow within 30 days;
- support, review or model cost removes a viable margin;
- regulatory or ownership boundaries cannot be documented.
