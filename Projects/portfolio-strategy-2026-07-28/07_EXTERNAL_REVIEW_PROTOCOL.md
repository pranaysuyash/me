# External review protocol

## What was and was not done

The requested `wide-open-brainstorm@carl-tools` plugin was unavailable, and external Codex, Claude Code, Kimi and Gemini agents were not directly callable. No external review is claimed.

The prompt pack is designed to produce genuinely independent reviews rather than four copies of the same summary.

## Required review roles

1. Commercial operator: speed to cash, offer, delivery and margin.
2. Venture/product strategist: recurrence, wedge, defensibility and market expansion.
3. Industry buyer: procurement, risk, workflow fit and objections.
4. Red-team skeptic: reasons the project should be killed.
5. Technical repo auditor: actual shipped evidence, architecture drag and launch blockers.
6. GTM specialist: reachable ICP, channel and message.
7. Pricing specialist: value metric, packaging and economic boundary.
8. Career reviewer: portfolio signal and role/client implications.
9. India operator: local buyer behavior, price, regulation and channels.
10. Global remote operator: exportable service/product and trust barriers.
11. Accessibility/safety/regulatory reviewer where relevant.
12. Synthesis judge: compare disagreements, evidence quality and action.

## Output contract

Every reviewer must provide:
- repository/file evidence;
- public sources when making market claims;
- score by the shared rubric;
- strongest disconfirming argument;
- next paid evidence test;
- stop rule;
- uncertainty and what would change the conclusion;
- no feature brainstorming until buyer/job is fixed.

## Ingestion

Save raw responses under `reviews/YYYY-MM-DD/raw/<reviewer>.md`. The synthesis must preserve disagreement rather than averaging it away. Update the decision log only when new evidence changes status, allocation, price, wedge or kill criteria.
