# Executive synthesis

## The blunt answer

You do not have a shortage of promising projects. You have an **attention-allocation and evidence problem**.

The portfolio repeatedly optimizes for breadth, architecture, feature completeness and documentation before proving a buyer, acquisition route, willingness to pay or repeated use. That produces technically credible assets but weak commercial selection pressure. The correction is not to find one magical app. It is to operate a **barbell**:

1. **Cash engine:** DocumentOps Studio, using SignKit and MetaExtract as components, sold as a productized outcome before it becomes software.
2. **Compounding software bet:** insurance_q narrowed and renamed into a source-linked policy servicing workbench; the strongest adjacent enterprise wedge is Policy Change Intelligence.
3. **Paid-validation bet:** Waypoint’s inquiry-to-trip packet, sold into existing travel operations rather than as a complete operating system.
4. **Customer-gated options:** AdShot and SentinelTwin/FieldCanvas receive no significant build time until design partners pay or supply real workflows.
5. **Career/portfolio allocation:** RadBridge, Rigs Unbound, VerseSignal, ICML and selected visual/knowledge projects receive a protected but small budget.

## Recommended weekly allocation

| Lane | Allocation | What counts as work |
|---|---:|---|
| DocumentOps sales and delivery | 35% | prospecting, paid diagnostics, workflow mapping, customer delivery, reusable SOPs |
| SignKit productization inside DocumentOps | 15% | launch, pricing, vertical templates, batch/reuse, support loop |
| Policy Intelligence / insurance_q | 30% | safe narrow workflow, real policies, institutional design partners, paid proof |
| Waypoint paid validation | 15% | agency outreach, inquiry case studies, paid pilots, integration learning |
| Career/creative rotation | 5% | one selected public artifact or playtest, never general backlog work |

AdShot or SentinelTwin can replace Waypoint’s 15% only after a named buyer provides data and pays. They do not get parallel allocation.

## Strongest commercial conclusions

### 1. The best near-term money is not a new SaaS

The fastest route is a productized remote workflow service around **repeated, sensitive document completion**. SignKit alone solves a narrow task; MetaExtract alone exposes too much technical surface; insurance_q contains a domain workflow. Combined, they can produce a paid operational result for accounting, legal, HR, benefits and insurance teams.

### 2. Insurance is the strongest long-term founder advantage

The product must avoid recommendation, sale, procurement and claim-representation language. Basic electronic storage is not enough. The valuable layer is source-linked explanation, document change intelligence, servicing workflow, corrections, dates, contacts and auditability.

### 3. Waypoint is a better third bet than another consumer app

Travel agencies already buy software, have repeated inbound workflows and can be reached directly. The repository is overbuilt relative to the wedge. Sell the inquiry-to-trip packet and follow-up sequence. Hide the rest.

### 4. AdShot is a service hypothesis, not a defensible image generator

Low-cost specialist tools and commerce platforms already produce product imagery at scale. AdShot only earns attention if it owns product truth, compliance, approval, channel packaging and governed publishing. Three fixed paid launch packs are more valuable than another model integration.

### 5. SentinelTwin cannot lead with camera placement

Free vendor tools already plan coverage, storage and bills of materials. The viable wedge is vendor-neutral audit evidence, blind-spot analysis, replay, post-incident reasoning and reviewable reports. It is customer-gated.

### 6. Most remaining projects should be preserved but removed from the active queue

EchoPanel, PhotoSearch, AI Glossary, MetaExtract and Scene/media intelligence become capability families. ShopStack, Drape, ReLoop and personal/reflection apps pause. Rigs, VerseSignal, RadBridge and ICML are portfolio/career assets with explicit caps.

## Revenue scenarios are tests, not forecasts

The revenue bands in this report are **offer-design hypotheses**. They are not TAM claims and should not be quoted as forecasts. Revenue becomes credible only after paid diagnostics, paid pilots, repeat use, references and a repeatable acquisition path.

## The next irreversible decision

Do not build another project. Sell the first DocumentOps diagnostic, narrow insurance_q, and test Waypoint with real agency inquiries. The portfolio should earn the right to expand through evidence.
